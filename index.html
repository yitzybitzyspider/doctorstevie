I was born and raised in Parkland, Florida, with deep New York roots. I have trained across the country and internationally. I am grateful to care for everyone who walks through my door.

I am a clinical psychologist. I work with people across the lifespan in trauma recovery, grief and loss, mood, and the transitions that ask us to adapt. My training spans the VA healthcare system, oncology and interdisciplinary medical care, inpatient programs, outpatient private practice, and trauma recovery. I help people move beyond survival mode and build lasting skills for recovery, resilience, and a meaningful life.

I am an integrative psychologist. I look at the whole picture of a person. The mind, the body, relationships, and the life around them. I draw on modern, evidence-based approaches to help you understand how your past, your relationships, your health, and your everyday stressors shape how you feel today.

I earned my B.A. in psychology at George Washington University, with a double minor in public health and in human services and social justice. I went on to complete my doctorate in clinical psychology at Nova Southeastern University. I completed my clinical internship at VA St. Louis Health Care. I am currently finishing my postdoctoral residency at The Warren Alpert Medical School at Brown University.

Therapy is learning to grow more aware of yourself, and to find and understand the strategies that truly fit you. Some of these skills already live within you. Together, we harness and sharpen them.

This is collaborative work. Every person carries a story. A summation of experiences, layered and complex, with its own rhythm and sound. It is not lost on me what it means to sit alongside you, to hear that story, and to help you find your way through it. My work begins by honoring yours.

Beyond the Therapy Room

The soundtrack of your life

We are each a summation of our experiences. Every story has its own sound, like a song waiting to be heard. When a song lands with you in just the right way, at just the right moment, something inside you answers. That is my intention with you in session. I want to meet you exactly where you are.

The beat, the lyrics, the melody, and the instruments are different for every person. Finding them is a little like cooking, searching for the right ingredients until something comes together. It takes real time in the studio, and I bring curiosity, rhythm, and warmth to that search. Together, we listen for the patterns and the wiring that make you who you are.

This kind of work asks for patience and grace, with yourself and with the process. Change takes time, and it is rarely a straight line. I aim to be honest and transparent with you along the way, and yes, to leave room for humor too. Healing does not have to be heavy at every turn.

My work is rooted in a few quiet convictions. That courage and vulnerability are where real change begins. That dignity belongs to every person, in every chapter of a life. That we each hold the capacity to grow, to make meaning of where we have been, and to write the next verse of who we are becoming.

“Sometimes the bravest and most important thing you can do is just show up.”
