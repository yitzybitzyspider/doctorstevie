About

A little
about me

I'm a doctoral candidate in Clinical Psychology at Nova Southeastern University (Ph.D., 2026), currently completing my pre-doctoral internship at the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs in St. Louis — an experience that has deepened my commitment to serving those who've given so much.

Over the past several years I've had the privilege of working with veterans, children, adolescents, and adults across a wide range of settings: VA medical centers, neuropsychology clinics, pediatric outpatient practices, and trauma-focused research labs. My training spans neuropsychological assessment, trauma-informed therapy, CBT, and evidence-based group interventions.

Before psychology found me (or I found it), I was at The George Washington University studying psychology, founding student organizations, and learning how to listen. That curiosity about people has never left me — it just got a lot more letters after its name.
