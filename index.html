Born and raised in Parkland, Florida with strong New York roots, and training across the country, as well as internationally, I am grateful to provide care who walks into my door wherever I’ve been.

I completed my B.A. in psychology with a double minor in public health as well as human service and social justice at George Washington University in Washington, D.C. I attended Nova Southeastern University’s doctoral clinical psychology program where I developed a [ ] lens…

I completed my clinical internship at VA St. Louis Health Care and am currently completing my postdoctoral residency at The Warren Alpert Medical School at Brown University. My training has spanned across a variety of settings including: inpatient programs, outpatient mental health clinics, private practice, primary care clinics, trauma recovery programs, and Siteman Cancer Hospital. I specialize in life transitions and trauma across the lifespan. I collaboratively work with clients to create tailored goals related to trauma, identity, relationships, aging, grief/loss, and mood disorders.
